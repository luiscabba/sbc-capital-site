AED 12,500 review fee, once. The checklist arrives within 2 working days of registering. You upload at your own pace. Working answer, for SBC to confirm.

Two officers. Both must pass you.

Each officer reviews your whole file on their own. If either has a question, it comes back to you, never to a buyer. The review takes about 10 working days once your file is complete. Working answer, for SBC to confirm.

Pass or fall short, you know where you stand.

If you fall short

You are Conditionally Qualified.

  • You get an action plan listing exactly what to fix. SBC approves it.
  • Nothing reaches a buyer. You are not introduced until you are SBC-Qualified.
  • Resubmit within [re-check window]. [Resubmission fee terms]
  • Falling short is not shared with anyone.
If you pass

You are SBC-Qualified for 12 months.

  • SBC introduces you when a buyer's need fits.
  • Each introduction is approved by SBC on its own.
  • Passing is not a licence or a promised contract.

Twelve months, then a re-check.

The mark stays valid for a year. Then SBC checks your file again so it never goes stale.
