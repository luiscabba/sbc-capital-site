SBC-Qualified
It means
  • Identity, ownership, licences and sanctions checked
  • Two SBC officers approved
  • Re-checked every year
Hover to tilt · turn it over below
It does not mean
  • A government licence or an accreditation
  • A promised contract
  • An introduction to every opportunity
SBC decides each introduction on its own
