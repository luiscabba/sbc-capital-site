SBC-Qualified

What the mark means, and what it does not.

SBC
SBC-QualifiedSBC Capital · Dubai
Valid 12 months
It means
  • Ownership, sanctions, licences, agent and ICV checked
  • Two SBC officers approved
  • Re-checked every year
What it is notRead before you rely on it
It does not mean
  • A government licence or an accreditation
  • A promised contract
  • An introduction to every opportunity
SBC decides each introduction on its own.
